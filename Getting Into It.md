I use a bike as my main mode of transportation because it saves time walking between classes while still providing me with regular exercise. My decision to build a lighter, new bike was spurred by two things: 1) my experiences trying to bike up the hill to the School of Business at UCR, and 2) the fact that I was about to transfer to UCLA, a remarkably hilly campus. My old bike is 29 pounds and, in my experience, is incredibly difficult to climb inclines. 

The environment and purpose of biking are important factors in what kind of bike to build (if you want an efficient bike). You wouldn't typically use a pocket knife to cut down a redwood tree. I needed a bike that would be comfortable climbing hills with a 15 lb backpack, responsive, durable, and capable of braking on a dime. During this project, I asked loads and loads of people (and the internet) for advice.
